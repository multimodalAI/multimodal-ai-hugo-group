Dr. Hazrat Ali (SMIEEE, FHEA) is a Lecturer in AI at the University of Stirling. He leads the HAIMI Research Lab. His research on Generative AI and Healthcare AI is published in leading venues, including Nature Biomedical Engineering and IEEE Transactions on Image Processing. He is editor of two books, LLMs in Healthcare (CRC Press, 2026) and Advances in Deep Generative Models for Medical Artificial Intelligence  (Springer, 2023), lead organiser and chair of the Foundation AI Models in Biomedical Imaging (FAIBI) workshop series at IEEE ISBI, and serves as Area Chair at IEEE IJCNN and IEEE ICASSP conferences.
