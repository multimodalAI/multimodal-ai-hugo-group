UKOMAIN has selected two challenges for OMAIB Round 3 and invites [applications to the sandpit](https://forms.gle/ugpbMWuuMNY1TLL28) in Sheffield on **21–22 January 2027**, where participants will form teams and develop proposals for open multimodal AI benchmarks.

We are seeking complementary expertise for:

- Deep-ocean seafloor mapping, with the National Oceanography Centre (NOC): evaluating AI using sonar, underwater imagery and vehicle sensor data. Relevant expertise includes multimodal learning, acoustic data processing, data engineering, uncertainty quantification and efficient embedded computing.
- Antibody–antigen prediction reliability, with Oxford Protein Informatics Group (OPIG), University of Oxford: assessing which binding predictions can be trusted, including identifying incorrect poses and false positives. Relevant expertise includes structural biology, bioinformatics, protein design, software engineering, and AI evaluation and calibration.

[Read the selected challenge briefs.](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1loB9RS3UKkbPzHo9eST3OH307V-puBS3ob2PCFsagJ4/edit?usp=sharing)

We welcome UK-based researchers, engineers and domain specialists who can make a clear contribution to at least one challenge, including early-career researchers and suitably experienced students. Relevant expertise from other fields is welcome. Apply individually; attendance on both days in person is required.

Up to £50,000 per project (80% FEC) is available competitively for benchmark development. UKOMAIN will cover reasonable UK travel, accommodation and subsistence for selected participants.

Apply by 11 December 2026, 16:00 UK time via the [Sandpit application form](https://forms.gle/ugpbMWuuMNY1TLL28) 

Please read the [full call document](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1fVzXvcdsbOGKJTW2_8a00Rnp5tfaisoAkC86C8XqmyA/edit?usp=sharing) for eligibility, funding conditions and participation requirements.

Please share this opportunity with colleagues whose expertise could contribute to either challenge.
